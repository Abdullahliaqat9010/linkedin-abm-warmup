# Templates and examples

Examples use a fictional seller ("a tool that automates candidate screening")
and fictional prospects. Adapt the shape, never copy the words.

## Comments (Days 1–7)

Goal: show you thought about their post. Add something; ask nothing for
yourself.

Bad:
- "Great post, totally agree!"
- "Love this. We help companies with exactly this — DM me."
- "So true 💯"

Good shapes:
- **Add a specific point:** "The part about phone screens eating recruiter
  time matches what I see — the hidden cost is the no-shows, not the calls
  themselves. Did that change once you moved to async?"
- **Respectful counter-view:** "I'd push back slightly on 'more applicants is
  good' — past a point it's more noise per hire. Curious where you draw that
  line."
- **Concrete example:** "We tried the same thing with a 5-person team: the win
  was cutting the first round to 10 minutes, not removing it."

Rules: 1–3 sentences, reference something specific in the post, end with a
genuine question about half the time, no product mention.

## Connection notes (Days 8–14)

Default: send blank to anyone who has seen your comments.

If a note helps (they haven't seen you, or there's a strong shared thread):
- "Enjoyed your take on async first-round interviews — would be good to stay
  connected."
- "Saw you're building out the recruiting team at <Company> — following along."

Never: a pitch, a meeting ask, "I'd love to pick your brain", a link.

## Give-first assets (Days 15–21)

Must be specific to the company. Test: swap in another company's name — if it
still works, rewrite it.

Shapes:
- **Mini teardown:** "Went through your careers page while looking at
  <signal>. Three quick things I noticed: <1>, <2>, <3>. Happy to share how
  others handled #2 if useful."
- **Signal observation:** "Noticed you've opened 6 engineering roles since
  <date>. Teams that scale hiring that fast usually hit <specific bottleneck>
  around month two — <one line on how to get ahead of it>."
- **Targeted resource:** "<Resource> — sharing because section 3 covers
  <thing directly tied to their situation>."

Never: "Thought you'd find this interesting" with a generic link; anything
that's really a pitch.

## Day-21 DMs

Shape: signal or prior thread → why it matters → one low-friction question.
Under 60 words.

Direct:
> Hi <Name> — since you're hiring 3 recruiters this quarter, I'm guessing first-round screening is about to get heavy. We've been helping teams that size cut that step to 10 minutes per candidate. Worth a quick look, or not a priority right now?

Softer:
> Hi <Name> — following up on the careers-page notes. Curious: is screening volume on your radar this quarter, or is the bigger headache elsewhere?

Bad:
- Opening with "I'm <name> from <company> and we…"
- Three questions in one message
- "Do you have 30 minutes next week?" as the first ask

## Follow-up (5–7 days after the ask, once)

Add new value; don't "just bump":
> Saw <new thing: post, hire, launch>. Made me think of <specific angle>. No pressure either way — happy to share what's worked for similar teams if it's useful.
